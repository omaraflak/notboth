Currently, `Mux16` allows you to choose between two words. This is not always enough, so build the four-way version, and then eight-way version out of it.

* four-way: 2 selector bits and four candidate words.
* eight-way: 3 selector bits and eight candidate words.

Remember to reuse existing components.

sig: Mux4Way16(a..d[16], sel[2]) → out[16] · Mux8Way16(a..h[16], sel[3]) → out[16]
needs: 1.05, 1.07
short: Mux4Way16, Mux8Way16
---

Choosing between two things is not enough. Build the four-way first, then the
eight-way from it: two selector bits and four candidate words, then three bits
and eight. One winner either way.

Do not invent a new mechanism for this. Build it out of the two-way mux you
already have, in layers — and notice while you do that a selector bit is just
an address bit. That realisation is most of Part III.

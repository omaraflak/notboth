The last piece of the CPU, and the one that turns a calculator into a
computer. Your ALU reports two facts about its result: it was zero (`zr`) and
it was negative (`ng`). Your instruction carries a small field saying which
outcomes should cause a jump. Compare the two, and if they agree, tell the
program counter to load instead of increment.

That handful of gates is where every `if`, every `while`, every function call
and every loop in every program that will ever run on this machine comes from.

title: Register
sig: Register(in[16], load, clk) → out[16]
needs: 3.05

A Register holds on to a *word*: sixteen Bits side by side, sharing one `load` and one `clk`.

> You now have a way to store variables!

::: watch
Remember to uses buses, and not sixteen individual inputs/outputs!
:::

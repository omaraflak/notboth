---
num: 3.06
title: Register
sig: Register(in[16], load, clk) → out[16]
needs: 3.05
---

Sixteen Bits side by side, sharing one `load` and one clock. Every pin is the
one from 3.05 made sixteen bits wide, and it behaves identically: `load` high
takes `in` at the next edge, low keeps what is there.

Mechanical and quick, and the first component that would be recognisable to
someone who has only seen a computer from the software side. It is a variable.

title: A program in binary
sig: your instruction table → bits in the ROM, by hand
needs: 4.08
---

Write something tiny directly in machine code — add two numbers and store the
result — encode it by hand from your table in 4.01, load it into the ROM, and
run it.

Do this exactly once. It is the moment the hardware stops being a diagram and
becomes a machine, and it will teach you more about your instruction set in
ten minutes than an afternoon of staring at it. It is also the quickest way to
see why you want an assembler, which is the point.

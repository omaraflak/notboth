num: 1.06
title: DMux
sig: DMux(in, sel) → a, b
needs: 1.01–1.03
---

The mirror image: one input, two outputs, and the selector decides which one
receives it. The other goes quiet.

```truth
sel | in | >a | >b
0 | 0 | 0 | 0
0 | 1 | 1 | 0
1 | 0 | 0 | 0
1 | 1 | 0 | 1
```

Where a mux *gathers*, a demux *routes*. You will need it the moment you build
memory: storing a value at an address means delivering a write signal to
exactly one cell out of many and to none of the others. Addressing is
demultiplexing.

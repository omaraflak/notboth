Write a sieve, in your language, and print the primes below a thousand.

When it runs, trace what actually happened. A number you typed was compiled to
a stack language, translated to assembly, assembled into bits, stored in a ROM
you configured, fetched by a counter you designed, decoded by wires you
placed, and computed by an arithmetic unit that is, at the bottom, some
thousands of copies of a single gate that only knows how to say “not both”.

There is no part of that chain you took on trust.

title: FullAdder
sig: FullAdder(a, b, c) → sum, carry
needs: 1.03, 2.01
---

A gate that adds *three* bits. This will be useful when we extend the adder over the entire 16-bits space, as we will need to sum two digits + a carry from the previous *column* (remember how to make an addition on paper?).

num: 2.02
title: FullAdder
sig: FullAdder(a, b, c) → sum, carry
needs: 2.01, 1.03
---

Add *three* bits. The third is the carry arriving from the column to the right
— the same thing you do by hand when you add 17 and 25 and put a little 1
above the tens column.

This is the unit cell of all your arithmetic, so build it out of half adders
rather than from scratch. Getting the carry-out right is the fiddly bit: think
about when a column produces a carry rather than trying to draw the circuit
first.

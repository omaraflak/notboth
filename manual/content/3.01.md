Every circuit so far is a pure function of its inputs. None of them have *states* or can remember.

To get there, the trick is to **connect a gate's output back to its own input.** A real gate takes time to respond, so the value arriving back is the value from *before*. A circuit that can see its own past can hold a state.

Before you continue, wire a NAND's output to its own input, and try to experiment and build intuition for what is happening.

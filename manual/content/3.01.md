title: The leap into time
sig: no new chip — an idea
needs: a clear head
---

Every circuit so far is a pure function of its inputs. None of them can answer
“what was it a moment ago?”, and a computer is nothing but a device for
remembering things.

The change is one wire: **connect a gate’s output back to its own input.** A
real gate takes time to respond, so the value arriving back is the value from
*before* — and a circuit that can see its own past can hold a state. Memory is
not a new kind of component. It is ordinary gates plus delay.

Do it on the bench before building anything: wire one gate’s output to its own
input, switch on, and watch. Then slow the clock right down and watch again.

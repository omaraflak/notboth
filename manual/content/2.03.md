Sixteen full adders in a row. This will mimic the extact steps you take when adding two numbers on paper.

Once your machine can add two 16-bit numbers, it can also (using two's complement in [2.05](/manual.html#s205)) subtract, compare, and count.

::: watch
Test the carry chain deliberately!
:::

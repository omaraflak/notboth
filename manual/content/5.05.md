Now design a language you would actually enjoy writing, and compile it down
one level. Variables, expressions, `if`, `while`, functions, and some way to
group data together. That is enough for real programs; resist adding more
until you have run something.

Because you are targeting the intermediate language rather than the metal,
each construct becomes a small, local translation — a `while` is a couple of
labels and a conditional jump, an expression is a walk over its own tree
emitting pushes. That keeps each construct small enough to hold in your head.

num: 1.07
title: Widen everything to sixteen
sig: Not16(in[16]) → out[16] · And16, Or16, Mux16
needs: 1.01–1.05
short: Widen to sixteen
---

Your machine will work on *words*, not bits — sixteen bits at a time is a good
size: big enough to address a useful amount of memory, small enough to wire
without losing your mind. So each of the gates you just built needs a wide
sibling that applies it to every bit position independently.

There is no cleverness here and that is the point: this is the first stage
that is purely mechanical, and it is where you will be grateful that pins have
widths and wires can carry a whole bus. Sixteen copies of a part, side by
side, sharing a control signal.

Note what `Mux16` is: a single selector bit choosing between two entire
numbers. That is the shape of nearly every control decision to come.

::: watch
Test the ends and the middle, not just `0000…` and `1111…`. A bus wired one
bit out of alignment passes both of those and nothing else.
:::

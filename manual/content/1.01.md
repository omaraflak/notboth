title: Not
sig: Not(in) → out

---

Negation, and the smallest possible proof that one gate is enough. You have a
part with two inputs and you need one with a single input, so the whole puzzle
is what to do with the spare.

```truth
in | >out
0 | 1
1 | 0
```

Once this exists you can stop thinking of NAND as “not-and” and start thinking
of it as raw material.

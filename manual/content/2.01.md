title: HalfAdder
sig: HalfAdder(a, b) → sum, carry
needs: 1.02, 1.04
---

Add two bits. There are only four cases and you can write them on your hand:
0+0, 0+1, 1+0, and 1+1 — which is two, which does not fit in one bit, which is
why there is a second output.

Look at the two output columns separately and you will find you built both of
these chips in Part I. That is the whole stage: arithmetic is not a new kind
of thing, it is logic looked at from a different angle.

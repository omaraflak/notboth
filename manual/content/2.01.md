num: 2.01
title: HalfAdder
sig: HalfAdder(a, b) → sum, carry
needs: 1.02, 1.04
---

Gate that adds two bits. There are only four cases: `0+0`, `0+1`, `1+0`, and `1+1`. Remember those are binary addition, but it works exactly like decimal additions:

* In decimal, when you go above `9` you loop back to `0`.
* In binary, when you go above `1` you loop back to `0`.

Therefore, just like in decimal, for two individual digits you will have a resulting `sum` and a `carry`.

::: answer
```circuit HalfAdder
in  a
in  b
out sum
out carry

and1 : And(a = a, b = b)
xor1 : Xor(a = a, b = b)

sum = xor1.out
carry = and1.out
```
:::

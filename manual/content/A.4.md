title: When you are stuck
---

Three questions, in order. *What exactly should this part do?* — write the
truth table before the circuit; half the time the answer appears while you
write it. *What do I already have?* — the piece you need has usually been
built already, two stages ago, under a different name. *Can I say it
differently?* — “a or b” became buildable the moment it was restated as “not
both off”, and that move works far more often than it has any right to.

And if none of that helps: build the smaller thing. A part that resists you is
nearly always two parts that have not been separated yet.

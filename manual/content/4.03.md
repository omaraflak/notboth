Now that you know the instructions format, let's implement a decoder chip that converts one 16-bit instruction into the control signals the rest of the CPU consumes.

```truth
.output | .source bit | .when `op` = 0
`zx nx zy ny f no` | `instruction[11..6]` | pass through unchanged
`isAddr` | `instruction[12]` | pass through unchanged
`loadA` | `instruction[5]` | forced to `1`
`loadD` | `instruction[4]` | forced to `0`
`writeM` | `instruction[3]` | forced to `0`
`j1 j2 j3` | `instruction[2..0]` | forced to `000`
```

> `writeM` is for `write memory` when `A` contains an address pointing to the RAM.

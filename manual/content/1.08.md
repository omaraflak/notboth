“Is anything at all switched on in here?” A single bit summarising a whole
bus.

It looks like a throwaway utility. It is not: this is how your machine answers
“is this number zero?”, and that question is the entire basis of conditional
branching. Sixteen wide because a number is sixteen wide — invert this chip’s
output and you have the ALU’s `zr` exactly, with nothing left over to build.
Every `if` and every `while` in every program you ever write on this machine
will come down to a chip like this one looking at a bus and shrugging.

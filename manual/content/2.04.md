Add one. You could of course use the adder with a hard-wired 1, and you may —
but adding one is such a common operation that it is worth having its own name
in your library.

The reason is Part III. A program counter spends almost its entire life doing
exactly this, once per instruction, forever. Naming the operation now means
the counter reads as what it is instead of as a special case of addition.

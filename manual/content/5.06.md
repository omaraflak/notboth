Your hardware can add. It cannot multiply, divide, allocate memory or draw a
line. Those are library functions, written in your own language, and this is
the layer people forget to budget for.

Multiplication is repeated addition until you make it cleverer. Division is
the same in reverse. Memory allocation is bookkeeping over a region of RAM.
Each one is a small program, and writing them is the first time you get to
*use* the thing you built rather than build it.

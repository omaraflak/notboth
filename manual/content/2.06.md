---
num: 2.06
title: ALU
sig: ALU(x[16], y[16], zx, nx, zy, ny, f, no) → out[16], zr, ng
needs: 1.xx, 2.03–2.05
---

The arithmetic and logic unit: one box that computes any of a couple of dozen useful functions of two numbers, with control bits selecting which: add, subtract, invert, increment, bitwise-and, bitwise-or, pass one input through, produce a constant.

Six single-bit controls, acting in this order to compute the output `out`:

- `zx` — replace `x` with zero.
- `nx` — invert `x` (applies after `zx`).
- `zy` — replace `y` with zero.
- `ny` — invert `y`, (applies after `zy`).
- `f` — the operation: 1 `ADD`s the two pre-processed inputs, 0 `AND`s them.
- `no` — invert the answer (`NOT`) on the way out.

Two extra outputs matter as much as the number itself:

* `zr` says if the result is zero.
* `ng` says if the result is negative (c.f. two's complement).

::: watch
This is the largest chip so far. Test every control combination you intend to use, with at least one negative operand.
:::
